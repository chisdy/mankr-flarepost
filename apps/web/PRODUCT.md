# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Mankr Flarepost serves personal site owners who handle day-to-day mail on their own domain. Desktop and mobile web are equally important.

## Product Purpose

Receive, read, compose and reply to domain email in a lightweight, self-hosted web client. A single administrator can organize messages and manage mail delivery from the same application.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One account and one domain with no application limit on aliases. Includes search, stars, tags, inbound filters, rich text and automatic draft saving. Attachments are unsupported; affected messages show a notice. The worker uses Cloudflare Email Routing and D1, with outbound mail through a configured provider. English and Simplified Chinese are supported.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Mankr Flarepost name and the existing fold mark in `public/brand/`.
